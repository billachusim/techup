# WhatsApp Conversion Playbook (copy-paste scripts)

A sales-script document you can keep open on your phone or laptop and paste from, built around what people actually ask you: SIWES, courses, price, duration, certificates.

No changes to the website. The output is a document only.

## What you get

A single playbook delivered in two formats:
- A PDF (clean, printable, easy to read on the phone)
- A plain-text/Markdown version so you can copy blocks without formatting breaking in WhatsApp

## Structure of the playbook

1. **Golden rules** — reply speed, always end with a question, one offer per message, never send a price without value first, always ask for their name, school and department.
2. **Opening replies** (paste depending on their first message)
   - "I'm interested" / "Info please"
   - "How much?"
   - SIWES / IT enquiry
   - Course or bootcamp enquiry
   - Parent asking for a child
3. **Qualifying questions block** — 4 short questions that decide which offer they get (name, city, school/level, goal, online or physical, when they want to start).
4. **Offer scripts** — one paste-ready message per offer, with value first, price second, next step last:
   - Physical / hybrid / online course enrolment (learning mode + module pricing)
   - Virtual SIWES placement — ₦45,000
   - Logbook review, signing and two-way waybill — ₦15,000
   - SIWES Success Kit funnel and free checklist
   - Holiday bootcamp for JSS3/SS3 students (parent version)
5. **Objection handling** — "too expensive", "let me think about it", "is the certificate recognised?", "can I pay in instalments?", "is it online?", "will I get a job after?", "my school won't accept it".
6. **Answers to the repeat questions** — duration, certificate and verification link (techfaculty.ng/verify), campuses, accreditation (FMSTI via NBTI), employment record.
7. **Closing scripts** — asking for payment, sending account details, confirming payment, onboarding message.
8. **Follow-up sequence** — Day 0 / Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 7 / Day 14 messages for people who go silent, plus a re-activation message for old leads.
9. **Broadcast and status templates** — short posts for WhatsApp Status and the community group that pull people into DMs.
10. **Quick-reply cheat sheet** — a one-page table of trigger → paste this, for fast use during a busy chat.
