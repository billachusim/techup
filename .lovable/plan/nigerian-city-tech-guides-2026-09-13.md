# Nigerian City Tech Guides

## Goal
Publish a useful local guide for every Nigerian city currently represented in the Tech Faculty hubs directory, helping students understand the city’s tech scene, nearby hubs, suitable skills, and the clearest Tech Faculty study route.

## Coverage
Create 14 original articles for:

- Nnewi
- Onitsha
- Enugu
- Owerri
- Aba
- Abuja
- Lagos
- Port Harcourt
- Ibadan
- Kano
- Nsukka
- Jos
- Kaduna
- Ota

## Article structure
Each guide will include:

- A city-specific search title, description, slug, and introduction
- An honest overview of the local economy and technology opportunities
- The hubs already verified in the directory, linked to their individual pages
- Recommended course paths matched to local sectors and student needs
- Options for in-person, hybrid, online, SIWES, portfolio building, certification, and job preparation where applicable
- A short practical “how to start” roadmap
- City-specific FAQs
- Internal links to `/hubs`, relevant hub and location pages, `/departments`, `/virtual-siwes`, and `/careers`
- A WhatsApp link preloaded with the city and the student’s request for guidance

## Accuracy and positioning

- Independent hubs will be described as editorial listings, never as Tech Faculty partners.
- Only locations marked as Tech Faculty campuses in the directory will be presented as our physical locations.
- Cities without a Tech Faculty campus will direct students to local hub communities plus Tech Faculty’s online, hybrid, SIWES, and advisory routes.
- No unverified employment, salary, ranking, partnership, or programme claims will be added.

## SEO and publishing

- Add the 14 guides to the existing static blog library under the `Tech Careers` category.
- Keep one page-level H1 by leaving headings out of the article body where the page already renders the title.
- Give every guide self-referencing canonical and social metadata through the existing blog page system.
- Add every new article URL to the static sitemap with monthly change frequency.
- Cross-link the city guides to the existing Nigeria hubs overview so the articles form a discoverable local-content cluster.

## Verification

- Confirm all 14 posts appear in the blog data with unique slugs.
- Open representative campus and non-campus city guides in the preview.
- Check titles, descriptions, H1 count, local hub links, internal links, and decoded WhatsApp messages.
- Run the project’s existing checks and resolve any regressions caused by these additions.
